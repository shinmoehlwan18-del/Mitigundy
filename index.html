Flood in Yangon

Exploring the Yangon flood

Flooding in Yangon often occurs during the monsoon season due to heavy rainfall and poor drainage systems. It can damage homes, roads, and businesses while disrupting people's daily lives.

Flood in Thaketa

Exploring the Thaketa flood

Thaketa Township is particularly vulnerable to flooding because it is located between Pazundaung Creek and the Bago River. During the rainy season, heavy rainfall, rising water levels, and clogged drainage systems can cause frequent flooding, affecting homes and daily activities.

Flood in Hlaing Tharyar

Exploring the Hlaing Tharyar flood

Hlaing Tharyar Township experiences flooding during periods of heavy monsoon rainfall. Low-lying neighbourhoods are especially vulnerable, as floodwater can enter homes, disrupt transportation, and affect residents' livelihoods.

STAY PREPARED

Flood Safety Essentials

Knowing what to do before, during, and after a flood can help protect lives and reduce damage.

Before a Flood

Preparation is essential to keeping yourself and your family safe.

  • Check weather forecasts and flood warnings.
  • Prepare an emergency bag.
  • Identify safe evacuation routes.
  • Keep important documents protected.
  • Clean drains and avoid blocking waterways.

During a Flood

Staying alert and following safety instructions can help prevent dangerous situations.

  • Move to higher ground when advised.
  • Follow official emergency instructions.
  • Avoid walking or driving through floodwater.
  • Stay away from electrical equipment in wet areas.
  • Keep in contact with family members.

After a Flood

Recovery requires caution to protect your health and ensure your surroundings are safe.

  • Return home only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid damaged buildings and power lines.
  • Drink clean and safe water.
  • Clean and disinfect affected areas safely.
  • Report damage and seek assistance if needed.
