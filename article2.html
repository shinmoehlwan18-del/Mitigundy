Myanmar - 15,000 Evacuate Floods in Bago, Mon and Yangon

On the 6th of October 2023 Bago township in the Bago Region. Myanmar's Department of Meteorology and Hydrology reported heavy rain in the township including a record October high of 20mm in 24hours which beats the previous record of 146mm set in October 2018. The water levels of Bago river which runs throughs the town. According to the authorities as many as 12,000 people were evacuated and around 2,800 homes have suffered damage. floods has also affected areas of Yangon region such as in Taikkyi Township about 100 people were displaced and a total of 27 houses were damaged.
