Prevention Tips

Thaketa Township Flood Prevention Tips

Before a Flood

  • Check rainfall, flood and high-tide warnings before leaving home.
  • Clean the drains around your house and remove leaves, mud and rubbish.
  • Never throw plastic, bottles or rubbish into drains, because blocked drains can worsen flooding in Thaketa.
  • Store important documents, phones and electrical items in waterproof bags and keep them in a higher place.
  • Move furniture, food and other valuable items to higher areas if flooding is expected.
  • Prepare an emergency bag with drinking water, food, medicine, a flashlight, batteries and important documents.
  • Know the safest route to a higher area or evacuation shelter.
  • Check on elderly people, young children and people with disabilities in your neighbourhood.

Protect Your Home

  • Raise electrical appliances and important belongings above expected floodwater levels.
  • Keep drainage channels around your house clear.
  • Do not build or place objects that block the flow of water in drains.
  • Use temporary barriers such as sandbags around entrances if flooding is expected.
  • Keep household waste in proper bins instead of leaving it near drainage channels.
  • Work with neighbours to organize community drain-cleaning days, especially before the monsoon. Thaketa drainage projects have specifically identified blocked internal drains and the need for regular maintenance.

When Heavy Rain or High Tide Happens

  • Stay indoors if travel is not necessary.
  • If authorities issue an evacuation warning, leave early rather than waiting for water levels to rise.
  • Avoid floodwater because it may hide open drains, holes, debris or electrical hazards.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment with wet hands.
  • Keep children away from flooded streets, drains and creeks.
  • Keep listening to official weather and emergency announcements.
  • Help neighbours who may need assistance to move to safer areas.

After the Flood

  • Wait for authorities to confirm that it is safe before returning.
  • Do not drink untreated water after flooding.
  • Clean and disinfect areas affected by floodwater.
  • Wear protective footwear and gloves when cleaning.
  • Do not touch fallen electrical wires.
  • Report blocked or damaged drains to the relevant local authorities.
  • Remove rubbish from around drains only when it is safe to do so.
  • Work with the community to improve drainage before the next rainy season.
